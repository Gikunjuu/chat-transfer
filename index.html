Chat Transfer

Move your conversations between ChatGPT and Claude, or from one Claude account to another.

1 · Get your export

ChatGPT Settings → Data controls → Export data. A .zip arrives by email.

Claude Settings → Privacy → Export data. Several .zip files arrive by email: chats, Projects, memory, profile. Drop them all.

2 · Drop it here

Drop every .zip you received. The tool reads them in your browser and lists your chats and Projects. Nothing is uploaded.

3 · Paste in the new account

Tick what you want, copy, then paste into a new chat in the destination account. For a large move, add the text to a Claude Project as a file instead.
